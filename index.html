SCENE 4 · PID EXTRACTION & SAFETY CONTROL

4. Gentle Bioactive Extraction

TEMPERATURE LOCK   87.4°C   ·   RANGE 85–90°C
STIRRER · 350 RPM
SSR ONLINE — NORMAL HEATING

Why 85–90°C

1
PID control locks extraction between 85°C and 90°C, preventing volatile oil loss that a harsh 100°C boil would cause.
2
The magnetic stirrer rotates at 350 RPM to keep coarse herb particles suspended for even extraction.
3
A dry-heating safety interlock cuts the SSR automatically if water level drops — try the button on the diagram.
